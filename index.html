About Me

I am a PhD candidate in neuroscience, exploring the fascinating intersection between music learning and neuroplasticity using advanced MRI methods.

My research focuses on how musical training can shape and enhance brain function. Alongside my academic pursuits, I am a passionate musician, playing the trombone for over a decade.
